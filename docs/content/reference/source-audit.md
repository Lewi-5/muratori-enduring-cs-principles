## What the checks mean

The source audit records reachable pages and unresolved requests separately. Public Handmade Hero index entries can establish the boundaries of assigned segments. A public Computer, Enhance! page or table of contents can identify an episode, but it cannot establish that a reader has subscription access or that every historical duration remains visible.

Beej is the approachable C companion. Links target specific HTML chapters and, where practical, sections. HTML references have no printed-page numbering; the title and stable URL are the locator. Primary standards and manuals retain the more precise clauses, tables, and page references in the package reading tables.
